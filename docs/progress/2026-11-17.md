# Progress note: 17 Nov 2026 (FashionNet, part 3)

**Branch:** `improve/cnn-gradcam-2026-11-17` (continues the merged work of 15 and 16 Nov)

## What was done

- `src/fashion.py`: Grad-CAM (`grad_cam`), heat-map overlay, a six-panel "recipe" figure and an occlusion helper; two new smoke tests (heat-map shape/range/class, occlusion on a blank picture).
- Notebook 03: Grad-CAM step by step on one picture, one example per class, five mistakes explained with the heat maps of the predicted and the true class, and an occlusion test of the heat map on 1,000 test pictures.
- README: notebook 03 in the contents, results, a "Then versus now" section; all three notebooks are listed as ready.

## Results (from the executed notebook, CPU, seed 42; the explained CNN is the one of notebook 01)

- Test accuracy 0.867 (same as notebook 01).
- 263 of 1,000 test pictures have a completely empty Grad-CAM map.
- Mean drop of the predicted-class probability when a 7 x 7 square is covered: 0.080 at the heat-map peak, 0.116 at a random garment pixel, 0.059 at a random pixel anywhere.
- Conclusion kept in the notebook: the occlusion test does **not** support "the Grad-CAM peak marks the most important place" for this network; it is a crude test (one model, one seed, one square size).
- In an exploration with 500 pictures I also tried the first convolution layer and the pooled output as the explained layer; none of them made the peak beat a random garment pixel. Those runs are not part of the notebook.

## Checks

- Notebook executed top to bottom; smoke tests and notebook tests pass in a fresh virtual environment (see the PR for the final run).
- Every number in the notebook texts was compared with the notebook output.

## Doubts / open points

- Grad-CAM is only one of several explanation methods; Captum or other methods were not needed because Grad-CAM worked, but they might give different pictures.
- The reasons given in the notebook for the weak occlusion result (coarse map, max-pooling, spread evidence) are hypotheses and were not tested.
